Assignment mapping (lines 6037–6039):

| Binding | Assigned to | Window | Crossover? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| `delta_7d` | `state.p7d_delta` | 7-day | no |

The `else` arm (6045–6047) sets all three fields to `None`; it is a sibling
scope and does not (and cannot) reference the bindings.
